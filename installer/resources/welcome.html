Welcome to Clippy

Clippy keeps everything you copy, so you can find it and paste it again in a second.

  • Searchable clipboard history with images and files
  • Snippets, pinned items, quick slots and Keychain-protected secrets
  • Transform text or ask AI on anything you've copied or selected
  • Paste the text inside any screenshot or image

Clippy will be installed in your Applications folder and start right away. It lives in the menu bar — there's no Dock icon.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Your history stays on this Mac.
